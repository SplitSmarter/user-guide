# Groups, roles, and visibility

> 完整译文稍后补充。产品专有名词（Split Smart、Smart Ledger、Debt Sync）保持拉丁写法。

The person who creates the group is the admin. People added later start as members. An admin or coordinator can edit the group and manage settlements and budgets. A member cannot. A public group can be found while it accepts members. A private group is only for members and people invited in. See the English page for the full explanation.
