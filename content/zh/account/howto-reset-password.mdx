# Reset your password

> 完整译文稍后补充。产品专有名词（Split Smart、Smart Ledger、Debt Sync）保持拉丁写法。

On **Login**, choose **Forgot Password?**, enter the email, and send the link. Open it or paste the token, then set a new password of 8 to 64 characters with a letter and a number. See the English page for steps.
