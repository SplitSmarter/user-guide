# What the cap does

> 完整译文稍后补充。产品专有名词（Split Smart、Smart Ledger、Debt Sync）保持拉丁写法。

**Soft** warns and the expense still saves. **Hard** can refuse a new expense that would pass the cap. The cap follows each person’s share, not who paid. See the English page for the full explanation.
