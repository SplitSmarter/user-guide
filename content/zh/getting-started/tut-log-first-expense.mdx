# Log your first expense

> 完整译文稍后补充。产品专有名词（Split Smart、Smart Ledger、Debt Sync）保持拉丁写法。

Choose **Expense**. Set **Paid by** to Alice for ₹1000 and **Split Between** to ₹400 and ₹600. Bob then owes Alice ₹600. The **Add transfer** switch on that screen is not how you settle. See the English page for steps.
