# Split Smart Help

Split Smart is for spending with other people.

**Smart Ledger** records an expense: who paid, and who shares the cost. Those are different. The person who paid is not automatically the only person who owes.

**Debt Sync** turns that into who owes whom. Recording that money moved does not clear the debt. The expense still shows who owes whom.

## Start here

Follow one dinner from a new account through the first expense and the payment that is recorded:

1. [Create your account](/en/getting-started/tut-create-account)
2. [Create a group and invite people](/en/getting-started/tut-create-group-and-invite)
3. [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense)
4. [Settle balances](/en/getting-started/tut-settle-balances)

Budgets, Memories, and Places can wait. You do not need them to see who owes whom.
