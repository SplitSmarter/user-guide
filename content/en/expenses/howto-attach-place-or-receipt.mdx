# Attach a place or receipt

These are three different things on a spend.

- The **place** is the **Location** on the expense: your current spot, a saved place, or none. See [On an expense](/en/places/concept-place-on-expense).
- A **receipt** is a photo of the bill. It is not the place.
- A **merchant branch** is the location you pick after choosing a merchant on a transfer. See [On a transfer](/en/places/concept-place-on-transfer).

Save a location you will use again in [Save a place](/en/places/howto-save-a-place). A Memory is separate: [Attach memory to an expense](/en/memories/guides/howto-attach-memory-to-expense).
