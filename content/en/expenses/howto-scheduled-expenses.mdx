# See scheduled expenses

This is the list of repeating expenses, not the form that creates the rule.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in
- At least one expense was saved with a recurring rule. Creating that rule is [Set up recurring expenses](/en/expenses/howto-recurring-expenses).

## Steps

1. Open the scheduled list.
2. Choose a start date and an end date. Both are required. The end must be on or after the start, and the range cannot be longer than six months. A longer or reversed range is refused.
3. Each card is one repeating expense. It shows the amount, how often it repeats, and whether the schedule is active or paused.

## What good looks like

Dates already passed are expenses you can open. Dates still ahead are every repeat in the range that is not saved yet, not only the next one. A paused schedule shows the past expenses and no dates still ahead.
