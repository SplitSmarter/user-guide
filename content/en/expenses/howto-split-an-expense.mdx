# Split an expense

**Paid by** is who put the money in. **Split Between** is who shares the cost. They can be the same people, or not. See [Paid by vs sharers](/en/expenses/concept-paid-by-vs-sharers).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet and choose **Expense**.
2. Give the bill a name. A description is optional. Choose the currency for the amount, then enter the amount.
3. Under **Paid by**, add each person who paid and how much they paid.
4. Under **Split Between**, add each person who shares the cost and their share.
5. The shares should add up to the amount. For each person, net is the amount they paid minus their share. A positive net means they are owed. A negative net means they owe.
6. If you put the expense in a group, everyone under **Paid by** and **Split Between** must already be in that group, or you add the missing people to the group as you save. If you do neither, the save is refused.
7. Save.

The first time through a shared dinner is [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense).

## What good looks like

Open the expense. It shows who paid, who shares, and who owes whom.
