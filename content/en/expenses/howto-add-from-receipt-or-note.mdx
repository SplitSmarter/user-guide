# Add an expense from a receipt or a note

A photo of the bill and a written note use the same draft. Nothing is saved until you confirm and save the expense. The draft is always a single date, never a repeating expense.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in

## Steps

1. Add one or more photos of the bill, write a note, or do both. A note can stand alone. A photo can stand alone. Photos you send stay with the draft as the receipt.
2. Review the draft before you save. It can fill a name, the amount, the currency, the date, a category, the line items, who paid, and who shares. It can also suggest a place and a group.
3. Check **Paid by** and **Split Between**, change anything that is off, then save it the same way as any other expense.

## What good looks like

Open the saved expense. The amount, the people, the lines, and the receipt photos match what you confirmed. Until you save, there is no expense.
