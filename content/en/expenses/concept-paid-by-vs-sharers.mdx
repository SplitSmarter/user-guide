# Paid by vs sharers

**Paid by** is who handed over the money. **Split Between** is who shares the cost. They can be the same people, or not.

For each person on an expense:

**Net = amount paid − share**

- A positive net means they are owed money.
- A negative net means they owe money.

## Dinner

Alice pays ₹1000. Alice’s share is ₹400. Bob’s share is ₹600. Bob paid nothing.

| Person | Paid | Share | Net |
|--------|------|-------|-----|
| Alice | ₹1000 | ₹400 | **+₹600** (owed) |
| Bob | ₹0 | ₹600 | **−₹600** (owes Alice) |

That is why the saved expense says Bob owes Alice ₹600. Paying the restaurant does not by itself mean Alice’s share was the whole bill.

On the expense form these are the **Paid by** and **Split Between** sections. The first time you set them is [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense). How the debt is cleared is [Balances and transfers](/en/settlements/concept-balances-and-transfers).
