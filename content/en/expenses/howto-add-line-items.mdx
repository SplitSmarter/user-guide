# Add line items

A line item is one thing on the bill, such as a dish. You can say who paid for that line and who shares it. If you leave a line unassigned, everyone already on that side of the expense shares it.

## Prerequisites

- You are on the expense form, including a saved expense you are editing

## Steps

1. On the expense, open **Assign items**.
2. Add the lines from your item list. You can add a new item if it is not there yet.
3. On **Paid by**, assign who funded each item. Leave a line empty to use everyone already under **Paid by**.
4. On **Split between**, assign who shares each item. Leave a line empty to use everyone already under **Split Between**.
5. The expense total follows the items. Save the expense.

## What good looks like

Open the expense. **Line items** lists each line, its amount, and who shares it.
