# Change a saved expense

## Prerequisites

- The expense is already saved
- You can open it

## Steps

1. Open the expense and change what you need: the name, the description, the amount, the currency, the date, the category, the group, the place, who paid, who shares, the lines, and the receipt photos.
2. Save. Saving recomputes what each person still owes.
3. If the change would remove money already applied to the bill, confirm that before the save finishes.
4. If someone else saved the same expense while you had it open, your save is refused. Open the expense again.
5. Changing the date follows [Attach memory to an expense](/en/memories/guides/howto-attach-memory-to-expense).

## What good looks like

Open the expense. The new amount and the people match what you saved, and what is still owed matches that split.
