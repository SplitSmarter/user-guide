# Create your account

You need an account before you can log a shared expense. There is no guest entry.

## Prerequisites

- An email address you can open
- Or a Google account, if you prefer that

## Steps

1. Open Split Smart. The first screens are **Social & Collaborative**, **Real-time Tracking**, and **Easy Settlement**.
2. Choose **Signup**.
3. Enter your name and password, and choose your country.
4. Enter your email. Split Smart sends a code.
5. On **Verify Code**, enter that code.
6. You land on the home wallet.

To use an account you already have, choose **Login**. [Log in with Google](/en/account/howto-login-with-google) is on the same start. If you forget the password, use [Reset your password](/en/account/howto-reset-password).
