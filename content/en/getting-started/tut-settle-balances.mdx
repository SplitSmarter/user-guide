# Settle balances

This continues the dinner you just logged. Alice paid ₹1000. Her share is ₹400. Bob’s share is ₹600. **Bob owes Alice ₹600.**

Recording that money moved does not clear that debt.

## Prerequisites

- The dinner expense from [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense)
- You are signed in as Bob, the person who owes, so the transfer can be sent as **You**

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet and choose **Transfer**.
2. Set the amount to ₹600.
3. Leave the sender as **You**. Set the recipient to Alice, the person who is owed.
4. Pick a category so the form can save.
5. Save the transfer. The dinner expense still shows Bob owing Alice.

The **Add transfer** switch on the expense form is different: that one pays a merchant on the bill. See [On an expense](/en/settlements/concept-add-transfer-on-expense).

## What good looks like

- A transfer exists from you (Bob) to Alice for ₹600.
- The dinner expense still shows that Bob owes Alice.
