# Settle balances

Clear what you owe after a shared expense. You will record that money moved, then link it so Debt Sync updates remaining debt.

This walkthrough uses a simple dinner: Alice paid ₹1000; Alice’s share ₹400, Bob’s ₹600 — so **Bob owes Alice ₹600**.

## Prerequisites

- An active Split Smart account (you will be on one side of the transfer — sender or receiver)
- A group with at least one shared expense (see [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense) if you need one)
- Custom users cannot be the sender on a transfer

## Steps

1. Open balances for the group or expense and confirm who owes whom (here: Bob owes Alice ₹600).
2. [Record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer) from **Bob → Alice** for ₹600. Sign in as Bob (you send) or as Alice (you receive) — **you must be one side** of the transfer.
3. On that expense, create a **settlement link** between the transfer and the expense for the full ₹600. See [Link a payment to an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense) (Path A: settle peer debt).
4. Check balances again. Remaining peer debt between Bob and Alice on that expense should be **₹0**.

## What good looks like

- The transfer exists with Bob as from and Alice as to, and you are one of those two.
- A settlement link ties that transfer to the expense.
- Bob no longer owes Alice for that bill (or the remaining amount matches any partial settle you chose).

Recording the transfer alone is not enough — without the settlement link, balances stay the same.
