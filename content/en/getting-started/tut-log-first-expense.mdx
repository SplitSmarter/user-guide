# Log your first expense

Log the dinner in Smart Ledger. Alice paid ₹1000. Her share is ₹400. Bob’s share is ₹600. After you save, **Bob owes Alice ₹600**.

Who paid and who shares the cost are different. See [Paid by vs sharers](/en/expenses/concept-paid-by-vs-sharers).

## Prerequisites

- The dinner group from [Create a group and invite people](/en/getting-started/tut-create-group-and-invite)
- You are signed in as one of the people on the bill

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet and choose **Expense**.
2. Select the dinner group.
3. Set the amount to ₹1000 and the date to today.
4. Under **Paid by**, set Alice as the person who paid the ₹1000.
5. Under **Split Between**, set Alice’s share to ₹400 and Bob’s share to ₹600.
6. Leave Place off. On a bill dated today, leave Personal Memories off if this dinner is not part of one. A Group or Shared Memory that is already on is applied unless you turn it off before you save.
7. Save.

Do not use the **Add transfer** switch on this screen. That switch records a payment to a merchant on the bill. It does not settle what Bob owes Alice.

## What good looks like

Open the expense. Bob owes Alice ₹600. **Settlement Path** still shows that amount as pending.
