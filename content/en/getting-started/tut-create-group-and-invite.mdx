# Create a group and invite people

A group is the shared ledger for this dinner. Create it before you log the bill.

## Prerequisites

- An account you are signed in to

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet.
2. Choose **Group** (shared ledger with people).
3. Enter a title, for example **Dinner**. The form’s examples are a weekend trip, flatmates, or office lunch.
4. Description and **Category** are optional.
5. Under **Members**, tap **Add** and choose the other person.
6. Tap **Create group**.

People who do not have an account yet, and the relationship you give them, are under [People](/en/people). Group roles stay under [Groups](/en/groups). You do not need either for this dinner.
