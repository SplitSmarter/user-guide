# Getting started

Use one dinner to see what Split Smart is for. Alice pays ₹1000. Her share is ₹400. Bob’s share is ₹600. After the expense is saved, **Bob owes Alice ₹600**. Recording Bob’s payment does not clear that.

## The path

1. [Create your account](/en/getting-started/tut-create-account) so you can sign in. There is no guest entry.
2. [Create a group and invite people](/en/getting-started/tut-create-group-and-invite) for this dinner.
3. [Log your first expense](/en/getting-started/tut-log-first-expense) with Alice as the person who paid and both shares set.
4. [Settle balances](/en/getting-started/tut-settle-balances) by recording the ₹600 payment from Bob to Alice.

When you are done, that transfer is saved, and the dinner expense still shows that Bob owes Alice.
