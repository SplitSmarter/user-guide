# Add a person

You add a person from the home wallet. Saving needs both the person and a relationship.

## Prerequisites

- An account you are signed in to

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet.
2. Choose **Person**. The screen asks **Who is this relationship with?**
3. Search by name, phone number, or email.
4. Pick their account if they are already on Split Smarter. If they are not, choose **Add “…” as custom contact**.
5. Choose a **Relationship**, such as Friend or Family. You can create a new kind from that screen if none fits.
6. Save. Save stays unavailable until both the person and the relationship are chosen.

A custom contact is still your person, with that relation. What happens after they join is in [Custom contacts](/en/people/howto-custom-contacts).
