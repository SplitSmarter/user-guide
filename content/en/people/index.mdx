# People

**People** are the contacts you split with. A group is the shared ledger. People are separate from that ledger.

- A **relationship** is the kind of connection, such as Friend or Family.
- A **relation** is that kind applied to one person. You choose it when you add them.
- A **custom contact** is someone who does not have a Split Smarter account yet. You still give them a relationship.
