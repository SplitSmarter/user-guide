# Relationship and relation

A **relationship** is the kind of connection. Split Smart includes Friend, Roommate, Colleague, Family, Partner, Travel Buddy, and Other. You can also create your own, with a title, a short description, and an icon.

A **relation** is one of those kinds applied to one person. Adding someone always includes a relation. You cannot save the person and choose the kind later.

The label is yours. Friend on your side does not by itself mean they have labeled you. What that difference allows is in [What a connection allows](/en/people/concept-connection-settings).

How to apply one: [Add a person](/en/people/howto-add-a-person).
