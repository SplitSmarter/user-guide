# Settlements (Debt Sync)

**Debt Sync** tracks what people owe after shared spending in **Smart Ledger**, and helps you clear those balances. Recording that money moved is separate from actually sending money with **Payments & UPI**.

## Start here

1. [Balances and transfers](/en/settlements/concept-balances-and-transfers) — how balances form and how transfers relate to debt.
2. [Record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer) — log that money moved between people or a merchant.
3. [Link a payment to an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense) — attach that transfer so debt clears or the bill shows as funded.

New to Split Smart? Follow the first-success path: [Settle balances](/en/getting-started/tut-settle-balances).

Want to send money in-app? See [Pay a friend](/en/payments/howto-pay-a-friend) after you understand how Debt Sync links payments to expenses.
