# Settlements (Debt Sync)

A **transfer** is a record that money moved. It can be between you and another person, or between you and a merchant. Saving it on its own leaves the expense as it was. Open the expense and settle that payment so the covered amount moves. See [Settle an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense).

You start one in two places:

- On its own, from the home create sheet, the home **Transfer** button, or **Activity** on the **Transfers** tab. See [Record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer).
- On an expense, with the **Add transfer** switch, which pays a merchant. See [On an expense](/en/settlements/concept-add-transfer-on-expense).

How the dinner bill becomes “Bob owes Alice” is [Balances and transfers](/en/settlements/concept-balances-and-transfers). How that payment covers the bill is [Settle an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense).
