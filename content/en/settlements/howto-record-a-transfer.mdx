# Record a transfer

Log that money moved between people, or between a person and a merchant. Recording a transfer does **not** clear balances by itself — link it afterward if you want Debt Sync to update what people owe.

## Prerequisites

- An active Split Smart account
- Who paid whom (or which merchant), and how much
- Optional: the group this transfer belongs with

## Steps

1. Open transfers in Split Smart and start a new transfer.
2. Choose **from** and **to**. **You (the current user) must be on one side** — either you paid, or you received. The other side is another person or a merchant.
3. Each side is either a person **or** a merchant (not both on the same side).
4. Enter the amount, date, and payment category. Add a group or description if it helps you find it later.
5. Save the transfer.
6. Confirm it appears in your transfer list with the correct parties and amount.

## Limits to know

- **You are always a party.** You cannot log a transfer that is only between two other people or two merchants; one side is always you.
- **Custom users cannot be the sender.** If someone without a full account paid, they cannot be on the “from” side.
- For **settle-up**, use a person → person transfer in the direction of the debt (who owes → who is owed), with you as either sender or receiver. Merchant transfers are for funding or refunds, not peer settlement.
- Saving the transfer only records cashflow. Open [Link a payment to an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense) to clear debt or mark bill cover.

## What good looks like

The transfer shows the right from/to, amount, and date. Balances on related expenses stay the same until you create a settlement or payment link.
