# Record a transfer

Use this when you want a record of money that already moved. It does not clear what people owe on an expense. See [Does not clear a debt](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense).

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in
- Who the money moved between, and how much

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet and choose **Transfer**. The same form opens from the **Transfer** button on home, or from **Activity**, on the **Transfers** tab, via **Add transfer**.
2. **Sender** starts as **You**. You stay on one side. Set the other side under **Select Peer**. The tabs are **People** and **Merchants**. The swap arrows trade the two sides.
3. Enter an amount greater than zero. The date starts as today. You cannot pick a future date.
4. Pick a category so the form can save. This guide does not explain those choices.
5. Optionally set a **Group**, a **Transfer Name**, or an attachment. If you leave the name blank, Split Smart names it from the recipient.
6. If a side is a merchant, you can pick that merchant’s branch, or skip it. The branch is [On a transfer](/en/places/concept-place-on-transfer).
7. Save.

A custom contact can be the person who was paid. They cannot be the person who paid.

## What good looks like

The transfer appears under **Activity**, on **Transfers**, with the amount and who it was from and to. If you set a group, it also appears on that group’s **Transactions** tab. Opening the row does nothing. There is no screen to edit or delete it.

The related expense is unchanged.
