# Settle an expense

Saving a transfer records that money moved. It does not, by itself, cover what someone still owes on a bill. Open the expense and settle that payment so the covered amount moves.

## Prerequisites

- The expense is saved
- You are signed in as the person who owes, when you are covering your own share

## Steps

1. Open the expense. Under **Settlement Path**, the amount still owed stays open until it is covered. Before any payment is applied, that section says **No settlement links on this expense yet.**
2. If you already recorded a payment with that person, choose **Use Transfer**. On **Settle**, that payment is listed under **From past transfers**. Use **Autoselect** or **Choose**, then **Settle**.
3. Or, on **Settle**, choose **New transfer**, enter the amount, pick a payment category, and choose **Settle**.
4. **Settlement Path** then shows **Settled** or **Partially Settled**, and the covered amount.

A transfer you save on its own, and never settle against the expense, still leaves the bill open. See [Record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer).

**Add transfer** on the expense form pays a merchant. It is not this step. See [On an expense](/en/settlements/concept-add-transfer-on-expense).

## Remind someone

On a group, **REMIND** opens **Send Payment Reminder**. **Custom WhatsApp Text** opens WhatsApp. **Send Anonymous Push** sends a notification in Split Smarter. Reminding does not cover the amount.

## What good looks like

The expense shows the applied amount as settled. Anything still unpaid stays as remaining.
