# Does not clear a debt

A saved transfer does not clear the dinner debt. After Bob records ₹600 to Alice, the expense still shows that Bob owes Alice.

The expense screen has no control to tie that person-to-person transfer to the bill.

The **Add transfer** switch on an expense is a merchant payment, saved with the bill. It is not this tie. See [On an expense](/en/settlements/concept-add-transfer-on-expense).
