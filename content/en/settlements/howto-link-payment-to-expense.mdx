# Link a payment to an expense

Attach an existing transfer to an expense so Debt Sync either **clears peer debt** or **marks the bill as funded**. Pick the path that matches your goal.

## Which one do I need?

| Goal | Use | Transfer shape |
|------|-----|----------------|
| Someone paid you back (or you paid them back) for a shared bill | **Settlement link** | Person → person (debtor → creditor) |
| Record which payment funded who paid on the expense (usually to a merchant) | **Payment link** | Typically payer → merchant (refunds: merchant → payer only) |

If you have not logged the money yet, [record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer) first.

## Prerequisites

- An active Split Smart account
- An expense with shares / paid-by already set
- A transfer whose parties and remaining amount match what you want to link

## Path A: Settle peer debt

1. Open the expense (or balances) where someone still owes someone else.
2. Choose a **person → person** transfer in the right direction: the person who owes sends to the person who is owed. You must already be one side of that transfer (you paid or you were paid).
3. Create a **settlement link** between that transfer and the expense. Use the full remaining amount, or a smaller amount for a partial settle.
4. Confirm remaining debt on that expense drops for those people.

**What good looks like:** Remaining peer debt is reduced (or zero). The transfer still exists; part or all of its amount is now linked as settlement.

Settlement links need peer transfers only — no merchant on either side. You (or a group admin/coordinator) must be allowed to link for that expense and transfer.

## Path B: Cover who paid the bill

1. Open the expense and the person listed as having paid.
2. Choose a matching transfer:
   - **Pay-out:** that person → merchant (their payment funded the bill)
   - **Refund:** merchant → that person only (reduces cover; cannot go below zero)
3. Create a **payment link** against that person’s paid-by on the expense. Link the full room available, or a partial amount.
4. Confirm cover toward their paid amount increases (or refund cover reduces net cover correctly).

**What good looks like:** The expense shows that paid-by as covered (fully or partly). This does **not** mean other people paid that person back — use Path A for settle-up.

## Shared amount pool

Settlement links and payment links draw from the **same** transfer amount. You cannot link more than the transfer still has available. Prefer separate transfers when one payment is purely settle-up and another is purely merchant cover.
