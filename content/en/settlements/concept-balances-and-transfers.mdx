# Balances and transfers

Debt Sync answers two questions after you log expenses: **who owes whom**, and **how do you clear that debt** when money actually moves.

## How balances form

Every shared expense has people who **paid** and people who **share** the cost. For each person on that expense:

**Net = amount paid − share**

- Positive net → they are owed money (creditor)
- Negative net → they owe money (debtor)

**Example:** Alice pays ₹1000 for dinner. Alice’s share is ₹400; Bob’s share is ₹600.

| Person | Paid | Share | Net |
|--------|------|-------|-----|
| Alice  | ₹1000 | ₹400 | **+₹600** (Bob owes her) |
| Bob    | ₹0    | ₹600 | **−₹600** (owes Alice) |

Group balances are those nets summed across the group’s expenses. For paid-by vs sharers detail, see [Paid by vs sharers](/en/expenses/concept-paid-by-vs-sharers).

## What a transfer is

A **transfer** records that money moved: person to person, or person to merchant (or the reverse).

**You (the signed-in user) are always on one side** — as sender or as receiver. The other side is another person or a merchant. You do not log transfers that involve only other people.

Recording a transfer alone does **not** change who owes whom. Debt Sync only updates balances when you **link** that transfer to an expense.

## Two kinds of links

| Link | What it does | Clears “who owes whom”? |
|------|----------------|-------------------------|
| **Settlement link** | Attaches a **peer** transfer (person → person) to an expense to clear debt | Yes |
| **Payment link** | Attaches a transfer to someone’s **paid-by** on the expense (usually payer → merchant) so the bill shows as funded | No — cover / receipt, not settle-up |

Continuing the example:

- Bob pays Alice ₹600 and you add a **settlement link** → remaining peer debt drops toward zero.
- Alice’s payment to the restaurant as a **payment link** → documents that her payment funded the bill; it does not mean Bob paid Alice back.

## One transfer, one amount pool

Each transfer has a single amount. Settlement links and payment links both draw from that same pool. As you link amounts, less of the transfer remains available to link elsewhere.

While a transfer has links, you typically cannot change its money or parties until those links are adjusted.

## Things to keep in mind

- **Settle-up needs a peer transfer.** Both sides must be people (no merchant), and you are one of them. Direction must match the debt: the person who owes sends to the person who is owed.
- **You can settle part of a debt.** Link less than the full remaining amount when needed.
- **Editing an expense after links exist** may scale, clamp, or unlink those links so the ledger stays consistent.
- **“Expense settled” is not the same as “everyone’s peer debt is zero.”** An expense-level settled flag can reflect linked settlement amounts against the bill total; peer balances are what Debt Sync clears person to person.
