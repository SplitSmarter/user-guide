# Balances and transfers

After you log a shared expense, Debt Sync shows **who owes whom**.

## How balances form

Every shared expense has people who **paid** and people who **share** the cost. For each person on that expense:

**Net = amount paid − share**

- A positive net means they are owed money.
- A negative net means they owe money.

**Example:** Alice pays ₹1000 for dinner. Alice’s share is ₹400. Bob’s share is ₹600.

| Person | Paid | Share | Net |
|--------|------|-------|-----|
| Alice  | ₹1000 | ₹400 | **+₹600** (Bob owes her) |
| Bob    | ₹0    | ₹600 | **−₹600** (owes Alice) |

For paid-by versus sharers, see [Paid by vs sharers](/en/expenses/concept-paid-by-vs-sharers).

## What a transfer changes

A transfer records that money moved. See [Record a transfer](/en/settlements/howto-record-a-transfer). The expense still shows who owes whom until you settle that transfer against the bill. See [Settle an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense).
