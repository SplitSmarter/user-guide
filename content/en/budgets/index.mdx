# Budgets

A **budget** is a cap on spending for a stretch of time. You name it, set the amount, and choose who it covers: **Just me**, **Shared**, or **Group**.

Create one in [Create a budget](/en/budgets/howto-create-a-budget). What the cap watches, and the difference between a warning and a block, is [What the cap does](/en/budgets/concept-budget-rules-and-caps).

## Where it shows up

A **group** budget appears on that group’s **Budgets** tab: the name, how often it resets, **Soft** or **Hard**, and the amount. Opening that row is not available yet.

A budget for **Just me** or **Shared** does not get its own list. While you add an expense, a matching budget can show as a chip with its name and how full it is. A hard cap that this expense would pass shows **Hard** on that chip.

The **Budgets & Goals** cards on home are examples. They are not the budgets you saved.

After you save, the app has no screen to edit, delete, or pause a budget.
