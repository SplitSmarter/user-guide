# What the cap does

The cap is the amount you set in [Create a budget](/en/budgets/howto-create-a-budget).

## Soft and hard

**Soft** is the start. It warns around 80% and at the cap. The expense still saves.

**Hard** can refuse a new expense that would go past the cap. It does not stop a transfer. Changing an expense later is not checked again.

## What it watches

The cap follows each person’s **share** of an expense, not who paid. See [Paid by vs sharers](/en/expenses/concept-paid-by-vs-sharers).

With nothing selected under **What counts**, those expense shares count. Picking **Categories**, **Places**, **Merchants**, or **Memories** narrows that. A parent category also covers the categories under it.

Money you send a person does not count. Money you send a merchant counts when that merchant is one you picked. If that payment is the same expense, it is not added again on top of the share.

**Just me** uses your shares. **Shared** also uses the shares of people who have joined. **Group** uses the shares of people in that group, on expenses in that group.

## When it resets

**Daily**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**. **Monthly** is the start. **Forever** means there is no fixed end date. A dated window, such as **This month**, ends when that range ends.
