# Create a budget

A budget needs a name and an amount. Everything else can stay as it is.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in

## Steps

1. On the home wallet, open the create sheet and choose **Budget**. The same form opens from **Create New** under **Budgets & Goals**. On a group, open **Budgets** and choose **Create budget**. That path already sets **Who** to **Group** and fills the group.
2. Enter a **Budget name**, such as Groceries.
3. Enter a **Spending cap** greater than zero. The currency shown is yours. You do not pick a different one.
4. Leave **Cap strength** on **Soft** unless you want a hard cap. See [What the cap does](/en/budgets/concept-budget-rules-and-caps).
5. Under **Who**, keep **Just me**, or change it.
   - **Shared** needs at least one other person. **You** stay on the budget. If that person only accepts an invite, they are not included until they accept. See [What a connection allows](/en/people/concept-connection-settings).
   - **Group** needs a **Target group**.
6. Under **What counts**, leave the tiles empty to watch expense shares in general. Or pick **Categories**, **Places**, **Merchants**, or **Memories** to narrow it.
7. **Resets** starts on **Monthly**. You can choose **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**. The window starts on **Forever**. Tap it and choose **This month**, **90 days**, or **Custom**. The start can be in the past. The end stays open, or is today or later. The end cannot be before the start. **Apply window** saves that choice. Spending already inside a past start counts toward the cap.
8. Choose **Create budget**.

You can also start from a saved place. **Budget** there puts that place under **Places** and fills the name. If the place is not saved yet, save it first.

## What good looks like

The form closes after the budget is saved. A group budget then appears on that group’s **Budgets** tab. A personal or shared budget does not appear in a list of its own.
