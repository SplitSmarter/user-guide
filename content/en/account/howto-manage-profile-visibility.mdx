# Who can find you

**Hidden** means other people cannot discover you. You do not show up when they search for someone to add. See [Add a person](/en/people/howto-add-a-person).

This is not the same as a group being public or private. That is [Groups, roles, and visibility](/en/groups/concept-groups-roles-visibility). Who may add you to a group, a Memory, or a budget is [What a connection allows](/en/people/concept-connection-settings).
