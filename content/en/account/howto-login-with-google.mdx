# Log in with Google

Use this when Split Smarter already has your Google account.

## Steps

1. Open Split Smarter. The first screens are **Social & Collaborative**, **Real-time Tracking**, and **Easy Settlement**.
2. Choose **Login with Google**.
3. Pick the Google account. You land on the home wallet.

**Login** with email and password is the other way in. **Forgot Password?** is on that screen. See [Reset your password](/en/account/howto-reset-password).

A new account can also start from **Signup**, then **Google**. That fills your name and email. You still choose a country and a password before the account is saved. See [Create your account](/en/getting-started/tut-create-account).
