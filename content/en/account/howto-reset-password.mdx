# Reset your password

Use this when you cannot sign in with the password you remember.

## Steps

1. Open **Login**.
2. Choose **Forgot Password?**.
3. Enter the email on the account and choose **Send reset link**.
4. If an account exists for that email, a link is on its way. Open it on this phone, or choose **Enter reset token** and paste the token from the email.
5. On **New password**, enter the new password and **Confirm password**.
6. Choose **Update password**. You return to **Login**.

The new password needs 8 to 64 characters, with at least one letter and one number. The two fields must match.

If a link was already sent, check that email. If you have tried too many times, wait a few minutes. An expired or already used link needs a new one.
