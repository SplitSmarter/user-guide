# Account & privacy

An account is how you sign in. Create one in [Create your account](/en/getting-started/tut-create-account): **Signup** with email and a code, or **Login with Google**.

## In this section

- [Log in with Google](/en/account/howto-login-with-google) when you already use Google for Split Smart.
- [Reset your password](/en/account/howto-reset-password) from **Forgot Password?** on **Login**.
- [Who can find you](/en/account/howto-manage-profile-visibility) when your profile is **Hidden**, so other people cannot discover you.

Your **Profile** shows your name, email, currency, country, and phone. **Sign Out Account** returns you to **Login**.
