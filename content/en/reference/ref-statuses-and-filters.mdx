# Statuses and filters

These are the states you can see on an expense or a budget.

## An expense still owed

Open the expense. **Settlement Path** says **No settlement links on this expense yet** while nothing has been applied. The remaining amount is what is still owed.

## Covered after you settle

After you [settle an expense](/en/settlements/howto-link-payment-to-expense), **Settlement Path** shows the covered amount.

- **Partially Settled** means some of the amount is covered and some remains.
- **All Settled** means nothing remains on that path.
- **Settled** is the label on the covered amount.

A transfer you saved and never settled against the expense does not change these.

## Paid is not the same as settled

Money tied to what someone paid does not clear who still owes. The expense is settled when nobody still on it is owed or still owes.

## A recurring expense

The expense shows **Recurring**. **Schedule** shows **Active** or **Paused**, the date it runs from, and the next date. See [Set up recurring expenses](/en/expenses/howto-recurring-expenses).

## A budget that is running

A budget is running when it has no end date, or the end is today or later. It resets on **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly**.

A budget whose end has already passed is no longer running. The create form does not offer an end before today. See [What the cap does](/en/budgets/concept-budget-rules-and-caps).

## Activity filters

On **Activity**, the filters are **All**, **Groups**, **Expenses**, and **Transfers**.
