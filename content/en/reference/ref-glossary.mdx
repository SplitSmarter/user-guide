# Glossary

Short definitions of common Split Smart terms.

## Memory

A **Memory** is a time-bounded experience (for example a trip or event) with a start and end date. Scopes are **Personal**, **Group**, or **Shared**. When a Memory is active, it is selected on the next expense in that group, and you can turn it off before you save. You can attach photos to it and complete it when the experience ends. See [Memories](/en/memories/concept-what-is-a-memory).

## Place

A **Place** is a saved location. On an expense it is the Location of that spend. On a transfer it is a merchant’s branch. It is separate from a Memory. See [Places](/en/places).

## Smart Ledger

The expense experience for logging shared and personal spend, including paid-by, sharers, and splits.

## Debt Sync

Who owes whom after shared expenses. A transfer records that money moved. Saving it does not clear that debt.

## Transfer

A record that money moved between you and a person, or you and a merchant. On an expense, **Add transfer** pays a merchant and is saved with the bill. See [Settlements](/en/settlements).

## Budget

A spending cap for a stretch of time. It can cover just you, people you share it with, or a group. **Soft** warns. **Hard** can refuse a new expense that would pass the cap. See [Budgets](/en/budgets).

## Paid by

Who paid the expense initially—not necessarily who owes the final share.

## Sharers

People who share the cost of an expense.

## Group

The shared place for one set of people: expenses, settlements, members, and budgets. The person who creates it is the admin. See [Groups](/en/groups).

## Relationship

The kind of connection, such as Friend, Family, or one you create. See [Relationship and relation](/en/people/concept-relationship-and-relation).

## Relation

A relationship applied to one person. You choose it when you add them.

## Custom contact

A person you add before they have a Split Smart account. Linking them to their account later carries the history that used that placeholder. See [Custom contacts](/en/people/howto-custom-contacts).

## Account

How you sign in. **Signup** uses email and a code, or you use **Login with Google**. **Forgot Password?** sends a reset link. A **Hidden** profile means other people cannot discover you. See [Account & privacy](/en/account).
