# Groups

A **group** is the shared place for one set of people. The **title** is the name, such as Dinner. **Category** is the optional type on the create form. Description, icon, and background are optional.

Create one in [Create a group and invite people](/en/getting-started/tut-create-group-and-invite). Who those people are, and the relationship you give them, stays under [People](/en/people).

## Inside a group

- **Overview** — the group itself
- **Transactions** — expenses and transfers
- **Settlements** — what people still owe
- **Members** — who is in the group, and their role
- **Budgets** — caps for this group. See [Budgets](/en/budgets).
