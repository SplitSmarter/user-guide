# Groups, roles, and visibility

The person who creates the group is the **admin**. People added later start as **members**. A **coordinator** has the same extra actions as an admin.

You see a person’s role on **Members**.

## Who can do what

**Admin** and **coordinator** can:

- Edit the group
- Invite and add people
- Log expenses
- Approve members
- Manage settlements and budgets

A **member** does not edit the group, and does not manage settlements or budgets. A member can log expenses, invite, add people, and approve members only while the group leaves those on.

## Public and private

A **public** group can be found while it is still accepting members. A **private** group is only for members and people invited in. A new group starts public.

How someone joins is in [Invite members](/en/groups/howto-invite-members). Whether they join immediately or get an invite depends on their settings in [What a connection allows](/en/people/concept-connection-settings).
