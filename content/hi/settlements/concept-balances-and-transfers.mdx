# Balances and transfers

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

Net is amount paid minus share. Alice paid ₹1000, her share ₹400, Bob’s share ₹600, so Bob owes Alice ₹600. A transfer does not change that. See the English page for the full explanation.
