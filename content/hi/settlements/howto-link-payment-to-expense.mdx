# Does not clear a debt

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

Saving a transfer does not clear what people owe. The expense screen has no control to tie a person-to-person transfer to the bill. See the English page for the full explanation.
