# Split Smart सहायता

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

Split Smart is for spending with other people. Smart Ledger records who paid and who shares the cost. Debt Sync shows who owes whom. Recording that money moved does not clear the debt. Full detail is in English.
