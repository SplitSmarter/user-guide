# Paid by vs sharers

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync, UPI) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

**Paid by** is who handed over the money. **Split Between** is who shares the cost. Net = amount paid − share. In the dinner, Alice paid ₹1000, her share is ₹400, and Bob’s share is ₹600, so Bob owes Alice ₹600. See the English page for the full explanation.
