# Glossary

## Memory

A **Memory** is a time-bounded experience with Personal, Group, or Shared scope. See English [Memories](/en/memories/concept-what-is-a-memory).

## Group

The shared place for one set of people. The person who creates it is the admin. See English [Groups](/en/groups).

## Transfer

A record that money moved. Saving it does not clear what people owe. See English [Settlements](/en/settlements).

## Budget

A spending cap. **Soft** warns. **Hard** can refuse a new expense. See English [Budgets](/en/budgets).

## Place

A saved location. On an expense it is the Location. On a transfer it is a merchant’s branch. See English [Places](/en/places).

## Account

How you sign in. A **Hidden** profile means other people cannot discover you. See English [Account & privacy](/en/account).

## Relationship

The kind of connection, such as Friend or Family. A **relation** is that kind on one person. A **custom contact** is someone without an account yet. See English [People](/en/people).
