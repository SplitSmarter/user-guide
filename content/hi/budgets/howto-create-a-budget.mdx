# Create a budget

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

From home, choose **Budget**. Enter a name and a **Spending cap**. **Just me**, **Soft**, **Monthly**, and **Forever** can stay as they are. **Shared** needs another person. **Group** needs a group. See the English page for steps.
