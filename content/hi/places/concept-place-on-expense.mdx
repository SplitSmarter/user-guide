# On an expense

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

**Location** on an expense is **Current location**, a saved place, or **Add Loc** for none. It is not a Memory, a receipt, or a merchant branch. See the English page for the full explanation.
