# Invite members

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

Add people under **Members** while you create the group, or share the group link. They join immediately, or they get an invite they can accept or decline. Someone already in the group is not invited again. See the English page for steps.
