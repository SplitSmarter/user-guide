# Add a person

> अनुवाद प्रगति पर है। उत्पाद नाम (Split Smart, Smart Ledger, Debt Sync) Latin रूप में रहेंगे।

From the home create sheet, choose **Person**. Search, pick an account or **Add “…” as custom contact**, then choose a relationship. Save stays unavailable until both are chosen. See the English page for steps.
